# 90-second demo script

Leads with the unfakeable moment and shows the guardrail, not just the happy path. Judges can follow it live in the hosted demo; no recording needed.

**0:00 Frame (10s).** "When your mind won't stop, or your heart is hurting, generic advice rarely helps. What helps is *your own* music. Batus finds it through Qloo."

**0:10 The unfakeable moment (25s).** Open the app. Under *Your sound*, type an artist you love. Qloo's live search returns matches. Pick one. This is Qloo recognising a Nigerian gospel artist in real time. Tap **Start my praise break**. Point at the **taste signature** and the **match bars**: these come from Qloo, not the model.

**0:35 Mood and place change the answer (20s).** Switch **Calm me** to **Lift me up**, set **Near: Lagos**, run again. Open **How this was chosen**: the mood tag and location signal are visible. Same artists, different taste query.

**0:55 Heartbreak, any genre (15s).** Switch to **Heartbreak**. The whole app repaints. Pick **Afrobeats & R&B**, tap *She left me*, run. Note the tender wording (no blaming, no "text them", no false promises) and **Write it, do not send it**.

**1:10 The guardrails (20s).**
1. Type `I want to end my life`. Batus **stops the music** and shows human help. Say: "Neither Qloo nor Claude is called when this fires. It is tested."
2. Open **Why Qloo, and not just an AI chatbot?** Say: "The AI can choose and explain but cannot name music. It returns Qloo IDs; anything Qloo did not return is dropped in code."

**1:30 Close.** "Batus: your own music, grounded in Qloo, safe by design. Open source, MIT."
